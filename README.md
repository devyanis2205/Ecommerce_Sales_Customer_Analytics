# E-Commerce Sales & Customer Analytics Dashboard

## 1. Introduction

The E-Commerce Sales & Customer Analytics Dashboard is a data analysis and visualization project developed using Microsoft Power BI. The main purpose of this project is to analyze e-commerce sales data and present important business information through interactive charts, cards, and graphs.

This dashboard helps users understand sales performance, profit, customer behavior, product performance, payment methods, and regional sales in a simple and visual format.

## 2. Objectives

- To analyze e-commerce sales data.
- To calculate total sales, total profit, total orders, and total customers.
- To calculate Average Order Value (AOV) and Profit Margin %.
- To analyze sales and profit across product categories.
- To identify the top 10 products based on sales.
- To analyze customer segments and loyalty tiers.
- To understand sales trends over time.
- To analyze payment methods and payment statuses.
- To compare sales performance across regions and sales channels.
- To create an interactive dashboard for better business decision-making.

## 3. Tools and Technologies Used

- **Microsoft Power BI Desktop:** For data visualization and dashboard creation.
- **CSV Datasets:** For storing and importing e-commerce data.
- **Power Query Editor:** For data cleaning and transformation.
- **DAX (Data Analysis Expressions):** For creating measures and calculating key performance indicators.
- **Power BI Model View:** For creating relationships between tables.
- **GitHub:** For storing and sharing project files.

## 4. Dataset Description

The project uses four CSV datasets containing synthetic e-commerce data.

### Dataset Files

- **orders.csv:** Contains order information, sales amounts, profit amounts, quantities, discounts, regions, sales channels, order statuses, and delivery days.
- **customers.csv:** Contains customer information such as customer ID, age, gender, city, state, customer segment, signup date, and loyalty tier.
- **products.csv:** Contains product information such as product name, category, subcategory, brand, unit price, unit cost, and price band.
- **payments.csv:** Contains payment information such as payment ID, order ID, payment method, payment status, amount paid, currency, and transaction fees.

The datasets contain synthetic records created for educational and analytical purposes.

## 5. Methodology

- Imported the four CSV datasets into Power BI.
- Checked the data and corrected data types where necessary.
- Cleaned and transformed the data using Power Query Editor.
- Created relationships between the Customers, Products, Orders, and Payments tables.
- Created DAX measures to calculate key performance indicators.
- Designed interactive dashboard pages to analyze sales, profit, customers, and market performance.
- Added slicers to filter data by date, region, sales channel, product category, customer segment, and payment method.
- Applied a consistent dark navy blue theme to improve the dashboard's appearance and readability.
- Tested the dashboard interactions and visualizations to ensure the results respond to filters.

## 6. Dashboard Visualizations

The dashboard contains three main pages.

### Page 1: Key Insights

- **Total Sales Card:** Displays the total sales amount.
- **Total Profit Card:** Displays the total profit.
- **Total Orders Card:** Displays the number of unique orders.
- **Profit Margin % Card:** Displays profit as a percentage of sales.
- **Monthly Sales Trend:** Shows changes in sales over time.
- **Sales vs Profit by Category:** Compares sales and profit across product categories.
- **Orders by Status:** Shows the distribution of orders by status.
- **Sales by Region:** Compares sales performance across regions.
- **Slicers:** Allow users to filter data by order date, sales channel.

### Page 2: Sales & Profit Analysis

- **Sales by Year:** Compares sales performance across years.
- **Top 10 Products by Sales:** Identifies the ten products with the highest sales.
- **Profit by Customer Segment:** Compares profit across customer segments.
- **Discount vs Profit:** Helps analyze the relationship between discounts and profit.
- **Payment Status by Payment Method:** Compares payment statuses across different payment methods.
- **Slicers:** Allow users to filter data by product category, customer segment, and payment method.

### Page 3: Customer & Market Analysis

- **Sales by Customer Age Group:** Compares sales across customer age groups.
- **Customer Acquisition Trend:** Shows customer signups over time.
- **Sales by Channel:** Compares sales across different sales channels.
- **Average Order Value by Segment:** Compares average order values across customer segments.
- **Customer Loyalty by Segment:** Analyzes customer loyalty tiers across customer segments.

## 7. Results and Insights

The dashboard provides an interactive overview of e-commerce sales and customer performance.

It helps users:

- Monitor overall sales, profit, orders, and customer activity.
- Identify top-performing products and categories.
- Compare sales performance across regions and sales channels.
- Analyze profitability across customer segments.
- Examine the relationship between discounts and profit.
- Understand payment method usage and payment statuses.
- Explore customer acquisition trends and loyalty patterns.
- Use slicers to investigate specific time periods, regions, categories, and customer groups.

Actual findings, such as the highest-performing product, most profitable category, total sales, and most popular payment method, should be added after analyzing the completed dashboard.

## 8. Conclusion

The E-Commerce Sales & Customer Analytics Dashboard demonstrates how Microsoft Power BI can transform raw e-commerce data into meaningful visual insights.

The project provides an interactive way to analyze sales performance, profitability, customer behavior, product performance, and payment information.

This project helped develop practical skills in data cleaning, data transformation, data modeling, DAX calculations, data visualization, and dashboard design. It can be included in a beginner-level data analytics portfolio to demonstrate practical knowledge of Power BI.
